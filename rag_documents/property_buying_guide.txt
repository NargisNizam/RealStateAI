Property Buying Guide

Before buying a property, a buyer should consider the property location, total budget, property type, size, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, accessibility, nearby facilities, and future development.

The buyer should compare similar properties in the same area before making a decision. The purchase budget should include not only the property price but also applicable taxes, fees, documentation costs, renovation expenses, and other transaction costs.

A property inspection can help identify visible structural, electrical, plumbing, or maintenance issues.

Buyers should verify important property information and documents through the appropriate official authorities or qualified professionals before completing a transaction.

Property prices can vary significantly between cities and neighborhoods. Historical prices do not guarantee future price increases.
